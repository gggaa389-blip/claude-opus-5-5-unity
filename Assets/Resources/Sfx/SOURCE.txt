Gunshot recordings (added on the user's request after the synthesised shots sounded wrong), from
"The Free Firearm Sound Library" by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney,
CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), the prepared version hosted on OpenGameArt:
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
(archive "Prepared SFX Library.7z", downloaded 2026-10-28).

Each clip is one shot, trimmed by the build helper (silence before the shot cut, tail faded), mixed to
mono, high-passed (45 Hz; 110 Hz for the near sniper shot, which carried wind rumble), 48 kHz 16 bit,
peaks at -1 dBFS. The near clips are lightly compressed for punch.

  assault_rifle.wav      AK-47, single shot, near           (C_28P.wav)
  assault_rifle_far.wav  AK-47, single shot, mid distance   (C_31P.wav)
  shotgun.wav            Benelli Nova 12 gauge, near        (O_21P.wav)
  shotgun_far.wav        Benelli Nova 12 gauge, mid distance (O_17P.wav)
  sniper_rifle.wav       Tikka T3 .30-06 bolt action, near  (W_29P.wav)
  sniper_rifle_far.wav   Tikka T3 .30-06, mid distance      (W_24P.wav)
  pistol.wav             Walther PPQ 9 mm, near             (X_39P.wav)
  pistol_far.wav         Walther PPQ 9 mm, mid distance     (X_31P.wav)

The rocket launcher, the explosions and the other effects are still synthesised (Sfx.cs).
